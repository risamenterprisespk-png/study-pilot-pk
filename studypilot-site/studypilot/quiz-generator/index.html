Quiz Generator

Generate practice questions from your study material.

How to use the quiz generator

  1. Paste your study material, or upload a PDF.
  2. Choose how many questions you want, from 1 to 20.
  3. Press “Generate quiz”, answer the fill-in-the-blank questions, then check the answer list.

How it works

Key sentences are turned into fill-in-the-blank questions by hiding an important term. Paste at least a paragraph of notes for the best results.

Your input stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Tips for best results

  • Paste at least a few paragraphs so there are enough sentences to choose from.
  • Cover the answer list while you practise, and write your answers down before checking.
  • Delete or edit any question that looks awkward, since sentences are picked automatically.

Common uses

Self-test before an exam, turn lecture notes into active-recall practice, build a quick revision quiz for a study group, or draft starter questions that a teacher then reviews.

Limitations

Questions are fill-in-the-blank only. Key terms are chosen by how often they appear and how important they look, so some blanks may be less meaningful than others.

FAQ

How many questions can I make?

Up to 20, depending on how much text you provide.

Why do some questions look odd?

Sentences and blanks are selected automatically. Skip or reword any question that does not make sense.

Can I make multiple-choice questions instead?

Yes. Use the MCQ generator, which adds four options and an answer key.

Is the quiz saved?

No. It exists only on your screen, so copy it if you want to keep it.
