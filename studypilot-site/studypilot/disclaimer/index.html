Calculator results

Our age, percentage and GPA calculators use standard formulas. However, grading rules differ between schools, universities and countries. The GPA calculator uses an unweighted 4.0 scale, which may not match your institution’s method, so always confirm official figures with your school or registrar.

Study tools are not AI authors

The summarizer, quiz, notes, MCQ, question finder and essay outline tools analyse your text on your device. They do not understand meaning the way a person does. Summaries may miss important points, questions may be awkward and answer options may be unsuitable. Review and edit everything before you use it for revision, teaching or assessment.

PDF and file tools

PDF tools process files in your browser and results depend on your device and the file itself. The compressor converts pages to images, so text may no longer be selectable. PDF to Word extracts text only and does not keep layout, tables or images. Scanned documents are not supported. Always keep an original copy of important files.
