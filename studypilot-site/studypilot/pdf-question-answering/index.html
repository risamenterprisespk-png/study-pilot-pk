PDF Question Answering

Ask a question and get the matching passages from your document.

How to use the pdf question answering

  1. Paste your document text, or upload a PDF.
  2. Type your question in the question box.
  3. Press “Find answer” and read the best-matching passages from your document.

How it works

Your question is matched against every sentence in the document, and the best-matching passages are shown. If nothing matches, the tool says so rather than guessing.

Your input stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Tips for best results

  • Use specific words that appear in the document, such as names, terms and numbers.
  • Ask one thing at a time, and try rephrasing with the document’s own wording if nothing matches.
  • Read the passages around the result to make sure the context fits your question.

Common uses

Find a definition in a textbook chapter, locate where a policy or contract mentions a topic, check a fact in lecture notes, or jump to the right section of a long PDF without scrolling.

Limitations

The tool matches the meaningful words in your question against each sentence. It does not reason, calculate or write new answers, and it can only return what is already in the text.

FAQ

Why does it return passages, not a written answer?

It finds the relevant text in your document instead of generating new wording.

Why does it say nothing matched?

None of the meaningful words in your question appear in the document. Try different or more specific keywords.

Can it answer questions that need calculation?

No. It finds relevant passages but does not do maths or draw conclusions.

How many passages does it show?

Up to the three best-matching passages.
