Contact Us

We would like to hear from you. Whether you found a bug, want a new tool or have a question about how something works, send us a message.

Email: studypilottool@gmail.com

We read every message and aim to reply within a few business days.

What you can contact us about

  • Bug reports: a tool gave a wrong result, failed with an error, or did not work on your device.
  • Feature requests: a tool you would like us to add, or a change to an existing one.
  • Privacy questions: anything about how the site handles data. See our Privacy Policy first, then write to us if you need more detail.
  • Business and advertising enquiries: partnerships, sponsorships or press.

How to report a problem

To help us fix an issue quickly, include these details in your email:

  1. The name of the tool and the page address
  2. What you did and what you expected to happen
  3. Your browser and device, for example Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone
  4. The size of the file, if the problem involves a PDF or image

Please do not email us confidential documents. Our tools work without uploading anything, so we never need your files to investigate a problem.
