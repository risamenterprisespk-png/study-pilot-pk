About Study Pilot

Study Pilot is a free collection of calculators, PDF tools and study helpers built for students, teachers and freelancers. Every tool runs inside your web browser, so there is nothing to install, no account to create and no file to upload to a server.

Why we built it

Most online PDF and study tools ask you to sign up, limit you to a few uses a day, or send your documents to a remote server. That is a poor deal when the file is a transcript, an assignment or a scanned contract. Study Pilot takes the opposite approach: the work happens on your own device, and the tools are free to use as often as you like.

What you can do here

  • Calculators: an age calculator, a percentage calculator and a GPA calculator for the 4.0 scale.
  • PDF tools: merge PDFs, compress a PDF, turn images into a PDF and extract PDF text into a Word-compatible file.
  • Study tools: a summarizer, PDF question finder, quiz generator, notes generator, MCQ generator and essay outline generator.

How the study tools work

We want to be clear about what these tools are. The summarizer, quiz, notes and MCQ tools do not use a generative AI model. They analyse your text on your device, rank sentences by how important their words are, and turn the best ones into summaries, notes or fill-in-the-blank questions. This keeps your material private and the tools fast, but the results are a starting point, not a finished product. Always read the output and correct it before you rely on it.

Your privacy

Text you paste and files you select are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to us. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

How the site is funded

Study Pilot is free to use. To cover hosting and development costs, we may show advertising on the site. Ads never change how a tool works or what it produces.

Get in touch

Found a bug, have an idea for a new tool, or spotted a wrong result? We read every message. Visit the contact page or email studypilottool@gmail.com.
