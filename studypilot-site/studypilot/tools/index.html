Which tool should I use?

Use a calculator when you need a number: your exact age, a percentage or percent change, or your GPA on the 4.0 scale. Use a PDF tool when you need to change a file: merge several PDFs, shrink a large scan, turn photos into a PDF, or pull the text out of a PDF into a Word-compatible file. Use a study tool when you have notes or a PDF to learn from: summarize it, ask it a question, or turn it into notes, a quiz or multiple-choice questions.

All tools are free, need no sign-up and work on your device, so your files are not uploaded. If something does not work as you expect, check the FAQ or contact us.
