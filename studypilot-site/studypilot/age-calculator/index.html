Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months and days.

How to use the age calculator

  1. Pick your date of birth with the date picker, or type it in.
  2. Press “Calculate”.
  3. Read your exact age in years, months and days, along with the total days you have lived and the days left until your next birthday.

How it works

The calculator subtracts your date of birth from today's date and borrows days from the previous month when needed, which matches how people count age. It also shows total days lived and a birthday countdown.

Your input stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Tips for best results

  • Always enter the full date of birth (day, month and year), not just the year.
  • The result is calculated to today’s date, based on your device’s clock, so check that your device date is correct.
  • For school admissions, exams or scholarships, confirm how the organisation counts age. Many use a fixed cut-off date rather than today.

Common uses

People use an age calculator to fill in application forms, check eligibility for a scholarship or exam, plan for retirement or insurance milestones, or simply find out how many days until the next birthday. Teachers and parents also use it to check whether a child meets a school’s minimum age.

Limitations

The tool calculates age up to today only. It cannot work out age on a past or future date, and it does not account for different calendars such as the Hijri or Hebrew calendar.

FAQ

What if I was born on 29 February?

In non-leap years the tool treats your birthday as 1 March.

Can I calculate age on a specific date?

Not yet. The calculator always measures from your date of birth to today’s date.

How accurate is the result?

It handles months of different lengths and leap-year birthdays, so the years, months and days match how people normally count age.

Is my date of birth saved anywhere?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
