Essay Outline Generator

Get a structured essay outline from a topic or prompt.

How to use the essay outline generator

  1. Enter your essay topic, or paste source text or notes.
  2. Press “Generate outline”.
  3. Copy the outline and fill each section with your own ideas and evidence.

How it works

Enter a topic or paste source text. The outline follows the standard essay shape: introduction with thesis, three supporting sections, a counterargument and a conclusion.

Your input stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Tips for best results

  • Be specific. “Effects of social media on teenage sleep” gives a better outline than “social media”.
  • Paste your notes if you have them, and the section headings will use the key terms from your text.
  • Turn the introduction into a one-sentence thesis before you start writing.

Common uses

Plan an argumentative or analytical essay, structure a school assignment, or organise a speech or presentation before you start drafting.

Limitations

It provides the standard essay structure: introduction with thesis, three supporting sections, a counterargument and a conclusion. It does not research your topic or write the essay, and you will need to adapt it for narrative essays or lab reports.

FAQ

Will it write my essay?

No. It gives you a structure to fill in with your own ideas.

Does it suit every type of essay?

It fits argumentative and analytical essays best. Adapt the sections for narrative, descriptive or scientific writing.

Can I use it for a presentation?

Yes. The same structure works for planning a talk.

Will it write the essay for me?

No. It gives you a structure so you can write the essay in your own words.
