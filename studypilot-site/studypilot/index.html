Why students use Study Pilot

Study Pilot is a free toolbox for students, teachers and freelancers who need a quick answer without creating an account. The age, percentage and GPA calculators give instant results, and the PDF tools let you merge, compress and convert documents in a few clicks.

Every tool runs in your own browser. Your PDFs, notes and images are processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers, which keeps assignments, contracts and personal files private. The study helpers use on-device text ranking, not a chatbot, so they stay within the material you give them: the summarizer picks key sentences, the quiz and MCQ generators turn your notes into practice questions, and the essay outline generator gives you a structure to write from.
