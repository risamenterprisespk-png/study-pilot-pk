Notes Generator

Turn long text or PDFs into structured study notes.

How to use the notes generator

  1. Paste your text, or upload a PDF.
  2. Press “Make notes”.
  3. Copy the headed bullet points into your notes app.

How it works

The tool finds the most important terms in your text, then groups the best supporting sentences under each as headed bullet points.

Your input stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Tips for best results

  • Longer, well-structured text produces better headings.
  • Rename the headings to match your syllabus or topic names.
  • Use the summarizer as well to get a short overview, then use these notes for detail.

Common uses

Turn a textbook chapter, lecture transcript or article into revision notes, or build a quick study guide from a long PDF.

Limitations

Headings are the most important recurring terms in your text, not true topics, and the bullets are sentences copied from your material. Always review the notes for accuracy and completeness.

FAQ

Can I copy the notes?

Yes. Select the result and copy it into your notes app.

How many headings will I get?

Up to six, based on the most important terms in your text.

Are the bullets written by the tool?

No. They are sentences taken from your own text and grouped under each key term.

Can I upload a PDF?

Yes, as long as it contains selectable text.
