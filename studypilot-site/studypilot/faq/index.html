Frequently asked questions

What if I was born on 29 February?

In non-leap years the tool treats your birthday as 1 March.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Choose % change and enter the old value as X and the new value as Y. A negative result means a decrease.

Does this support weighted GPA?

No. It uses the standard unweighted 4.0 scale.

Is there a file size limit?

No fixed limit, but very large files depend on your device's memory.

Why did my file get bigger?

Files that are already well optimized can grow. Try a lower quality setting.

Can I combine many photos?

Yes. Select multiple images at once.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

No. Scanned pages contain images, not text.

Is this an AI chatbot?

No. It uses on-device text ranking, so your text never leaves your browser.

Why does it return passages, not a written answer?

It finds the relevant text in your document instead of generating new wording.

How many questions can I make?

Up to 20, depending on how much text you provide.

Can I copy the notes?

Yes. Select the result and copy it into your notes app.

Are the wrong options reliable?

They come from your own text, so review them before using in a test.

Will it write my essay?

No. It gives you a structure to fill in with your own ideas.
