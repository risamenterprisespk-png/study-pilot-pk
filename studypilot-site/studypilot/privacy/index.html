Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Study Pilot (“we”, “us”) collects when you use studypilottool.com (the “Site”), and how it is used. By using the Site you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • Files you select and text you paste are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to or stored on our servers.
  • We do not require an account and do not ask for your name or personal details to use a tool.
  • We may show ads. If we do, advertising partners may use cookies, as described below.

Your files and text

All of our tools, including the PDF merger, compressor, image to PDF converter, PDF to Word extractor, summarizer, question finder, quiz, notes, MCQ and essay outline tools, work locally in your browser using JavaScript. The content you provide stays on your device. We cannot see it, and we do not keep copies of it.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the server that delivers the Site may automatically log technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request and the date and time of the request. This data is used to keep the Site secure, fix errors and understand overall traffic. It is generally handled by our hosting provider.

Local storage on your device

The Site stores a single preference in your browser’s local storage: whether you chose light or dark mode. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Open-source code libraries

To provide PDF and 3D features, our pages use open-source JavaScript libraries (pdf.js, pdf-lib and three.js). These files are hosted on our own website and are delivered together with our pages, so using the tools does not require your browser to contact a separate content delivery network for them. Like any website, our hosting provider receives standard request information such as your IP address when your browser loads a page or file.

Analytics

We do not currently use an analytics service. If we add one, we will update this policy and describe what it collects.

Advertising and cookies

We may display advertisements on the Site through Google AdSense or similar advertising services. If ads are shown, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to the Site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we will ask for your consent before using cookies for personalized advertising.

How we use information

We use the limited information described above only to operate and secure the Site, fix problems, understand general usage, and show advertising that helps fund the Site. We do not sell personal information.

Links to other websites

The Site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Children’s privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not collect account data or store your files, there is usually little to retrieve, but you can contact us at any time with a request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email studypilottool@gmail.com or use our contact page.
