Percentage Calculator

Work out percentages, ratios and percent change.

How to use the percentage calculator

  1. Choose what you want to work out: “X% of Y”, “X is what % of Y”, or “% change from X to Y”.
  2. Enter your two numbers in the Value X and Value Y boxes.
  3. Press “Calculate” and read the result.

How it works

Three formulas cover most needs: X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y; X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100; percent change = (Y − X) ÷ X × 100. Example: 20% of 150 is 30, and going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.

Your input stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Tips for best results

  • For a discount, find X% of the price, then subtract it. 25% of 80 is 20, so the sale price is 60.
  • For exam marks, use “X is what % of Y”. 42 out of 60 is 70%.
  • For growth or decline, use “% change”, with the old value as X and the new value as Y. Going from 50 to 65 is a 30% increase.

Common uses

A percentage calculator helps with shopping discounts and sales prices, exam and assignment scores, salary or price changes, and the statistics you quote in reports and presentations. Students use it to check homework, and freelancers use it to adjust quotes.

Limitations

Percent change cannot be calculated from zero, because dividing by zero is undefined. Results are rounded to four decimal places.

FAQ

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Choose % change and enter the old value as X and the new value as Y. A negative result means a decrease.

How do I find a price after a discount?

Calculate the discount as X% of the price, then subtract it. For 20% off 150, the discount is 30, so you pay 120.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

Going from 10% to 15% is a rise of 5 percentage points, but a 50% increase in the rate itself. Use the % change option for the second.

Why do I get an error when X is zero?

Percent change divides by the starting value, so it is not defined when that value is zero.
