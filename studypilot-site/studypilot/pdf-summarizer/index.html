PDF Summarizer

Summarize a PDF or text into key points.

How to use the pdf summarizer

  1. Paste your text into the box, or upload a PDF.
  2. Press “Summarize”.
  3. Read the overview and the list of key points.

How it works

The summarizer scores each sentence by how many of the document's important words it contains and returns the top sentences in their original order. It is extractive: it selects your own sentences rather than writing new ones.

Your input stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Tips for best results

  • Give it at least a few paragraphs. Very short text does not contain enough sentences to rank.
  • It works best on well-organised informational text such as articles, chapters and reports.
  • Check the key points against the original before quoting them, and remove headers and footers if they get picked up.

Common uses

Skim a long article before reading it in full, revise a chapter before an exam, decide whether a research paper is relevant, or get a quick overview of a lengthy report.

Limitations

The summary is extractive: it selects important sentences from your text and does not rewrite them, so some points may lack context. It is designed for English text, and scanned PDFs without selectable text cannot be read.

FAQ

Is this an AI chatbot?

No. It uses on-device text ranking, so your text never leaves your browser.

How long is the summary?

It returns between three and six key sentences, depending on the length of your text.

Can I summarize a scanned PDF?

No. The tool needs selectable text, and scanned pages contain only images.

Does it work in other languages?

It is designed for English. Other languages may give weaker results.
