GPA Calculator

Calculate your GPA on a 4.0 scale from grades and credit hours.

How to use the gpa calculator

  1. Add a row for each course you took.
  2. For each row, choose your letter grade and enter the credit hours.
  3. Press “Calculate GPA” to see your GPA and the total credits.

How it works

GPA is the sum of (grade points × credit hours) divided by total credit hours. An A (4.0) in a 3-credit course and a B (3.0) in a 4-credit course give (12 + 12) ÷ 7 = 3.43.

Your input stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Tips for best results

  • Include every course that counts toward your GPA, and leave out courses your school excludes, such as pass/fail.
  • For a cumulative GPA (CGPA), add the courses from all semesters in one go.
  • To plan ahead, add a hypothetical course with the grade you hope to get and see how your GPA would change.

Common uses

Students use a GPA calculator to check a semester result, work out a cumulative GPA for scholarship or graduate applications, and plan which grades they need next term to reach a target.

Limitations

The scale is the standard unweighted 4.0 scale: A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B- = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C- = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0 and F = 0. Schools with a different scale, or weighted grades for honours and AP courses, may give a different official GPA.

FAQ

Does this support weighted GPA?

No. It uses the standard unweighted 4.0 scale.

Is GPA the same as CGPA?

CGPA is your cumulative GPA across all semesters. Enter every course from every semester to get it.

Does it work for a 5.0 or 10-point scale?

No. It is built for the 4.0 scale. If your school uses another scale, use your school’s conversion chart first.

Do all courses count equally?

No. Courses with more credit hours count more, which is why you enter credit hours for each one.
